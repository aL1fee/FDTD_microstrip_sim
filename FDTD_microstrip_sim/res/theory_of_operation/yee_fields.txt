Yee fields and boundaries

* Yee staggering - Offset the electric and magnetic samples in space and time.
* Reflective boundary - An endpoint that returns part of the incident wave.

The 1D solver stores electric and magnetic samples in separate arrays. The magnetic sample H[i] represents the half cell between E[i] and E[i+1]. Each step updates E from neighboring H samples, applies sources and the selected experimental boundary, then updates H from neighboring E samples. The two fields are staggered by half a timestep.

With S = 0.5, the normalized update is:

    E[i] += S * (H[i-1] - H[i])
    H[i] += S * (E[i] - E[i+1])

The ordinary endpoint treatment reflects waves. The experimental boundary option applies a delayed neighboring E sample at the right edge when the Gaussian branch injects. Its behavior depends on the excitation and boundary history. It is an experimental treatment for the 1D demo.

Field amplitudes are normalized. The simulator currently demonstrates propagation and reflection in vacuum.
